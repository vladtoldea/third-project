Trial of the Astartes

The Trial

Before you can wear the armour of the Adeptus Astartes, you must prove your knowledge of the Space Marines. Answer ten questions about the Chapters, Primarchs, gene-seed and wargear of the Emperor's Angels of Death, drawn at random from fifteen. Every question has a countdown.

How to play

  1. Enter your name and press Begin the trial.
  2. Pick one answer before the timer runs out (15 seconds). You can also press 1 to 4.
  3. A correct answer earns 10 points, plus bonuses for speed and for unbroken streaks.
  4. Your best score and recent trials are recorded in this browser.
